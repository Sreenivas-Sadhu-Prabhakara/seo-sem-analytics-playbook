## Most people learn SEO. Almost nobody learns how to get *paid* for it.

That gap — between *knowing* how search works and *charging money* for it — is where this playbook lives. Every module is written as a stage of a **real freelance gig**, in the order you'd actually do it: land a client, diagnose their problem, wire up tracking, fix their search presence, run their ads, prove the results, and turn it into a retainer.

By the end you won't have "finished a course." You'll have **run a complete engagement for a real local business** — and you'll have the audit, the dashboard, the campaign, and the case study to show the next one.

## Who this is for

- **The absolute beginner** who's heard "SEO" pays and wants a real, sellable skill — read everything, skip nothing.
- **The marketer or freelancer** who knows the basics and wants to package search into a paying service — chase the `Practitioner` and `Freelancer` tagged sections.
- **The developer or shop owner** comfortable with tech who wants the marketing-and-money layer on top — the `Advanced` boxes are for you.

Every lesson is tagged **Foundations · Practitioner · Advanced** so you can read at your own altitude. One book, three reading paths.
