Welcome. Before you dive in, spend five minutes here — it's the difference between *reading a course* and *running a business*.

## The one rule that makes this work: the capstone client

Search marketing is a contact sport. You cannot learn it by reading, the same way you can't learn swimming from a poster. So here's the deal:

**In [Module 1](/seo-sem-analytics-playbook/modules/01-land-the-client/), you'll recruit one real local business as your practice client.** A cousin's boutique, a friend's dental clinic, your neighbourhood gym, your family's tuition centre — anyone with a real business and a weak online presence. Every assignment from Module 2 onward is done *for that client*.

By Module 12 you won't have notes. You'll have a live optimised Google Business Profile, a running ad campaign, a real analytics dashboard, and a **case study with actual numbers** — the single most powerful thing for landing your *next* (paying) client.

:::tip[No willing business? No excuse.]
If you genuinely can't recruit one, each assignment includes a **Simulated Client** fallback — a fictional-but-realistic brief you can work instead. It's Plan B, not Plan A. A real client, even an unpaid first one, is worth ten simulations.
:::

## Read at your own altitude: the level tags

This is one book with three reading paths. Sections and callouts are tagged:

- **🎓 Foundations** — for absolute beginners. If you already know what a keyword is, skim these.
- **Practitioner** — the core craft. This is the job. Everyone reads these.
- **🚀 Level up** — advanced plays for when you're comfortable. Beginners can safely skip on the first pass and circle back.

## The callouts you'll meet in every lesson

We hid the boring parts and kept the useful ones. Watch for these boxes:

:::tip[⚔️ War story]
A real-world tale — usually a mistake someone made so you don't have to. Named examples are **composites** (realistic but invented), never fake case studies dressed up as fact.
:::

:::note[📋 Swipe this]
A copy-paste template, script or checklist. Steal it, tweak it, send it. This is the stuff clients actually pay for.
:::

:::caution[⚠️ Gotcha]
A trap that catches beginners — and plenty of "pros." Read these twice.
:::

:::note[💰 Rupee check]
The money angle: what to charge, what a client will pay, what a tool costs. All figures are **illustrative ranges** — the Indian market moves, so always sanity-check current rates before quoting.
:::

And at the end of most lessons you'll find a **gut-check** — a few quick questions with the answers hidden. Try before you peek:

<details>
<summary>Q: What's the fastest way to see if you actually learned something?</summary>

Explain it to someone else — or to your capstone client — without notes. If you can't, re-read. This whole playbook is built to be *taught forward*.
</details>

## The 12-week map

The playbook is a comprehensive reference, but if you want a pace, treat it as a ~3-month program — roughly one module a week:

| Weeks | Modules | You're learning to… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **0 – The Landscape** | See how search turns into rupees, and set up your free lab |
| 2 | **1 – Land the Client** | Find, pitch and sign your first (practice) client |
| 3 | **2 – Audit & Discovery** | Diagnose a business's search problems like a pro |
| 4 | **3 – Strategy & Keywords** | Turn the diagnosis into a plan the owner buys |
| 5 | **4 – Measure First** | Install GA4 + Search Console *before* you touch anything |
| 6–7 | **5 – Local SEO & GBP** | Win the map pack — the fastest local money |
| 8 | **6 – On-Page & Content** | Build pages that rank *and* convert |
| 9 | **7 – Technical SEO** | Fix the plumbing Google trips over |
| 10 | **8 – Off-Page & Authority** | Earn links and reviews without getting penalised |
| 11 | **9 – Google Ads** | Buy traffic without setting money on fire |
| 12 | **10 – Optimize with Data** | Let the numbers tell you what to change |
| 13 | **11 – Report & Prove ROI** | Build the dashboard and report that keep you hired |
| 14 | **12 – Retain & Scale** | Flip one gig into recurring income |

:::note
Going faster? Great. Going slower? Also great. The capstone client keeps you honest either way — if you can't *do* the assignment, you're not done with the module, no matter how fast you read it.
:::

## What you need before Module 0

- A laptop, an internet connection, and a Google account (free).
- About 6–10 hours a week if you follow the 12-week pace.
- A shortlist of 3–5 local businesses you *could* ask to be your capstone client. Start thinking now — you'll pick one in Module 1.
