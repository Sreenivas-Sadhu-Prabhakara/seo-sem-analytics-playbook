Every good campaign starts with reconnaissance. Before you pick a fight, you scout the terrain — who's winning, who's weak, where the gaps are. In this assignment you'll do exactly that for a local niche, using nothing but a phone and the free public Google search everyone else has. By the end you'll have a shortlist of real businesses that could become your **capstone client** — the business you sign in Module 1 and practise on for the rest of the course.

This is the moment the course stops being reading and starts being a business.

## The brief

Produce a short **Market Recon** document that scouts one local niche and identifies two or three businesses that could become your capstone client. You'll pick a niche, search the way real customers do, record who's visible and who isn't, note each business's obvious weaknesses, and shortlist your best candidates.

## Steps / checklist

1. **Pick a candidate niche.** Choose one type of local Indian SMB you could realistically approach — dental clinic, gym, salon/parlour, coaching/tuition centre, restaurant or tiffin service, boutique, physiotherapy, packers and movers, whatever's around you. Prefer a niche where you know someone or can easily walk in. Write one line on *why* you picked it (personal connection, lots of them nearby, obviously weak online presence).

2. **Find 3 real local businesses** in that niche in your city or locality.

3. **Search like their customer.** For each business, run the **2–3 queries a real customer would actually type or speak** — and include at least one "near me" and one Hinglish/vernacular variant (e.g. "dentist near me," "best dental clinic [locality]," "sasta root canal near me"). For each query, record:
   - Who shows up in the **map pack** (the three businesses on the map)?
   - Who ranks in the **organic** results below?
   - Who's running **ads** (look for the "Sponsored"/"Ad" label)?
   - Does *your* business appear at all?

4. **Spot the obvious weaknesses** for each business. You're not doing a deep audit yet — just eyeballing the glaring gaps: no or unclaimed **Google Business Profile**, very few or no **reviews**, no website or an ancient/broken one, a site that's **slow on mobile**, no visible **WhatsApp/tap-to-call**, wrong or missing hours.

5. **Shortlist 2–3 candidates.** Mark which businesses are the best capstone prospects — ideally ones with clear weaknesses you could fix *and* someone you could plausibly reach. Weakness + approachability = your ideal first client.

## Deliverable format

A simple table or doc (a Google Doc, a spreadsheet, or plain notes) with one row per business:

| Business | Niche | Queries checked | Current visibility (map pack / organic / ads) | Obvious weaknesses | Capstone candidate? (Y/N) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *Sri Balaji Dental (example)* | Dental | "root canal near me"; "dentist Rajajinagar" | Not in map pack; ranks ~#8 organic; no ads | Only 6 reviews; no WhatsApp link; slow site | Y |

Keep it scannable. This is a working scout's note, not a polished report.

## Simulated Client fallback

No niche of your own in mind, or can't get out to scout? Work this composite instead (clearly hypothetical, but analyse it with real public Google search):

> **Niche:** dental clinics in **Jayanagar, Bengaluru** (or substitute a busy locality in your own city). Using only public Google search, run "dentist near me," "best dental clinic Jayanagar," and "root canal cost near me." Analyse the **three real clinics** that appear, record their map-pack/organic/ad visibility and their obvious weaknesses, and shortlist which two you'd most want as a capstone client and why.

You'll analyse real, publicly visible businesses — you're just not committing to approach them. It's genuine practice; a real niche you can actually reach is still better.

## Self-assessment rubric

Grade yourself honestly — own the numbers, remember?

| Criteria | Missing | Getting there | Client-ready |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Niche chosen & justified** | No niche, or "just picked one" | Niche picked, vague reason | Clear niche with a solid why (connection/volume/weakness) |
| **3 businesses analysed** | Fewer than 3, or not really searched | 3 listed, thin search notes | 3 scouted with real "near me" + Hinglish queries and visibility recorded |
| **Weaknesses identified** | None spotted | Generic ("needs better SEO") | Specific, observable gaps (unclaimed GBP, 4 reviews, no WhatsApp, slow site) |
| **Shortlist made** | No shortlist | Named but unjustified | 2–3 candidates with weakness *and* reachability reasoned out |

If any row is "Missing," you're not done with Module 0 — no matter how fast you read it.

## How this feeds your capstone

This shortlist is not busywork. **It's the pipeline for the client you'll sign in Module 1.** The businesses you scout here are your real prospects — you've already spotted their weaknesses, which means you already know what you'd pitch. When Module 1 teaches you to land the client, you won't be starting from a blank page. You'll be walking into a conversation you've already done the homework for. Scout well now, and your first client is half-signed already.
