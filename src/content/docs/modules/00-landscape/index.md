Before you can charge a single rupee, you need to see the battlefield.

Right now, somewhere within five kilometres of you, a woman is thumbing "best dentist near me" into her phone with a throbbing molar. A father is searching "CBSE maths tuition Kukatpally" at 11pm. A bride-to-be is typing "bridal makeup Jayanagar price." Every one of those searches is a person with a wallet, a problem, and an intention to spend — and most of the local businesses that could serve them are completely invisible. That gap, between demand that already exists and businesses too clueless to capture it, is the entire reason your future income exists.

This module doesn't teach you to *do* anything yet. It teaches you to *see*. See how a typed query turns into money in a shop owner's till. See the three tools you'll wield to redirect that money. See the peculiar, mobile-first, WhatsApp-driven, "near me" reality of Indian local search — which is not America with different currency, it's its own game. And then you'll build your free lab so that by the next module you're armed and dangerous.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

You won't be invoicing anyone off Module 0 alone — this is orientation, not a billable skill. But everything here is the foundation that lets you eventually charge for:

- **A "search visibility check"** — walking a business owner through where they *actually* show up (or don't) when their customers search. Owners find this genuinely eye-opening, and it's your foot in the door.
- **Explaining the SEO-vs-Ads trade-off** in plain Hinglish so an owner stops wasting money on the wrong one.
- **A market-recon report** that identifies which local businesses are leaving money on the table — the raw material for finding your first client.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Trace the value chain from a search query all the way to a sale and a referral — and explain why **leads, not rankings**, are the scoreboard.
- Describe SEO, Google Analytics (GA4), and Google Ads honestly, and say when each one earns its keep.
- Explain what makes Indian local search different, and why that difference is a massive opportunity for a freelancer.
- Stand up a complete, zero-cost toolkit and a safe "practice property" to learn on.
- Adopt the white-hat, own-the-numbers mindset that keeps clients paying you for years instead of firing you in month two.

## The deliverable

Your module Assignment is a short **Market Recon** doc: you'll pick a niche, search the way real customers do for three local businesses, log who's winning and who's invisible, and shortlist two or three that could become your **capstone client** — the real business you'll practise on for the rest of the course. Your shortlist is the client you sign in Module 1. This is where the whole gig begins.

## Tools and level

**Tools:** just a browser, a phone, and a free Google account to start. By the end of the module you'll have added Google Business Profile, GA4, Search Console, a Google Ads account with Keyword Planner, Looker Studio, Google Trends, PageSpeed Insights, and Tag Manager — all free.

**Level:** mostly **🎓 Foundations**, with a few **Practitioner** glimpses. If you already know a keyword from a KPI, skim the green boxes and slow down for the money and the market reality — that's where even experienced marketers get India wrong.
