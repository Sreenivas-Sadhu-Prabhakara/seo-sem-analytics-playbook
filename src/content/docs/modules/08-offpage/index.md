Everything you've done so far has been you talking about your client. The title tags, the service pages, the schema, the squeaky-clean site speed — all of it is your client standing on stage, saying "I'm the best physiotherapist in Indiranagar." Lovely. Confident. Google has heard it.

Off-page is when the *rest of the internet* walks up to the mic and says whether it agrees.

That's the whole game this module. On-page tells Google **what you are**. Off-page tells Google **whether the world backs you up**. Links, mentions, reviews and reputation are the votes and the gossip that decide "prominence" — Google's word for how well-known and well-regarded a business is. Two clinics can have identical websites; the one the neighbourhood, the local press and a physio association all point to is the one that wins the map pack.

Here's the catch that makes this the most dangerous module in the course: off-page is the one place a freelancer can get their client *penalised*. Every other skill, done badly, just underperforms. Done badly here, you can bury a business that was doing fine. So we're going to teach the durable, ethical, boringly-effective way to earn authority — and then walk you right up to the black-hat cliff and show you the bones at the bottom.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- An **off-page audit**: what's linking to the client, what it's worth, and what's radioactive.
- A **link & reputation plan** — the retainer-shaped work that keeps paying: local partnerships, digital PR, mention-hunting, outreach.
- **Reputation monitoring** as an ongoing service — knowing the moment someone mentions the client, for better or worse.
- **Backlink cleanup** for clients who inherited a toxic profile from a previous "SEO expert" (there are many; you'll meet their handiwork).

This is some of the stickiest retainer work you can sell, because authority is never "done." Links decay, competitors build, reviews keep coming. On-page you fix once; off-page you tend like a garden.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Explain honestly how link-based authority works in the current search era — and spot the myths that get sites punished.
- Build a realistic list of **relevant, local** link and mention opportunities for an ordinary SMB.
- Run genuine, non-cringe outreach that a busy local journalist or shop owner might actually answer.
- Design a linkable asset — a reason for people to link that isn't "please link to me."
- Monitor a brand's reputation across Google and the niche platforms that matter in India.
- Audit a bad backlink profile and know when (rarely) to reach for the disavow tool without blowing your foot off.

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is a **Link & Reputation Plan plus a first outreach batch** for your capstone client: an assessed current profile, 10–15 relevant local opportunities, one genuinely good outreach message, the first few sent or queued, and reputation monitoring switched on. It's a document a client would happily pay a retainer against.

## Tools and level

You'll use **Google Search Console** (the Links report), **Google Alerts**, a free backlink checker or two, **Google Business Profile**, and the Indian review platforms your client's customers actually use — JustDial, Sulekha, Practo, Zomato and friends. Mostly free. The level here is **Practitioner**, with a few **Advanced** flags for the disavow tool and heavier digital PR.

Reputation is earned, not bought. Let's go earn some.
