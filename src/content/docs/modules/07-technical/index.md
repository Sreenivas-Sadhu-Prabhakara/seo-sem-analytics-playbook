You can write the sharpest page copy in your city. You can earn five-star reviews and build the tidiest Google Business Profile in the pincode. But if Google's crawler can't reach the page, can't make sense of it, and it takes eleven seconds to load on a ₹200-a-month 4G plan in Nashik — none of that work counts. It's a beautiful shop with the shutter welded shut.

That's the ugly truth of this stage. Technical SEO is the plumbing. Nobody compliments you on the plumbing. Clients don't lie awake dreaming about their XML sitemap. But the day the plumbing bursts — the day half the site drops out of the index, or the homepage loads slower than the neighbour's — *that's* when they call you, panicked, and *that's* when knowing this stuff makes you look like a wizard.

This is the foundation everything else in the course sits on. Local SEO, on-page, content, ads — all of it pours down through the technical plumbing first. Get it right once and every other rupee you spend on the site works harder.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- A **standalone technical SEO audit** — a paid, one-shot project that finds why a site is crawling badly, loading slowly, or missing from Google, delivered as a prioritised fix list.
- **Speed and Core Web Vitals fixes** — the quick-wins that make a slow SMB site load like it respects the visitor's data pack.
- **Schema / structured-data deployment** — adding the machine-readable markup that makes a business eligible for rich results in search.
- **Platform clean-ups** — untangling a WordPress site with two fighting SEO plugins, or wringing the most out of a locked-down Wix or Shopify store.
- **Ongoing "site health" retainers** — the monthly check that catches an accidental `noindex` before it costs the client a month of traffic.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Explain, in plain Hindi-English, how Google **crawls, renders, and indexes** a page — and use Google Search Console to see exactly where a page is stuck.
- Use `robots.txt`, `noindex`, and canonical tags correctly (and know the difference, because mixing them up is how pros accidentally delete a client from Google).
- **Measure and improve site speed** with PageSpeed Insights and the Core Web Vitals report — and name the three metrics that actually matter (LCP, INP, CLS).
- Check a site for **mobile-first** reality on the phone-on-patchy-data user who is 90% of Indian local search.
- Write correct **LocalBusiness (and friends) structured data** and validate it, without violating Google's review-markup policy.
- Apply all of the above on the platforms you'll actually meet — WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Dukaan, one-page builders, and hand-coded sites.

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is a **before/after technical fixes checklist with evidence** — real PageSpeed scores, GSC indexing status, a mobile pass, and validated schema — deployed for your capstone client (or a prioritised fix plan if you're on the practice lab). It's the artefact that turns "trust me, I fixed it" into "here's the proof."

## Tools you'll use

Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, the Rich Results Test, Chrome DevTools (lightly), and whatever CMS your client is stuck on. All free. The only thing this module costs you is a couple of focused afternoons.

**Level:** Practitioner, with a few Advanced flags for the confident. Nothing here needs you to be a developer — but by the end you'll talk to developers like a peer instead of a nervous account manager.

Let's go fix the plumbing. First stop: making sure Google can even *find* the pages.
