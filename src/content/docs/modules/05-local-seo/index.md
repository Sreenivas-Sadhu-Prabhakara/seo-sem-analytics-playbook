You've landed the client. You've audited the site, agreed the strategy, and wired up GA4 so you can prove what you did. Now comes the stage that makes a client text you at 9pm saying "boss, phone hasn't stopped ringing." Welcome to **local SEO**.

Here's the truth most beginners miss: for a dentist, a salon, a coaching centre or a family restaurant, the website isn't the storefront. **The Google map pack is the storefront.** When someone in Indiranagar thumbs "dentist near me" into their phone, three businesses show up in a little box with a map, star ratings and a call button. That box — the *local pack*, the *3-pack*, the *snack pack*, whatever you call it — is where the money is. Above it are ads. Below it, the ten blue links almost nobody scrolls to on mobile. The three businesses in the middle catch the intent, the taps and the walk-ins.

This is also where you, the freelancer, look like a genius fastest. On-page SEO and link building take months to move. A neglected Google Business Profile, cleaned up and optimised properly, can climb in **weeks** — sometimes days. Visible wins early buy you trust, and trust is what converts a one-off project into a monthly retainer.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **Google Business Profile optimisation** — a one-time setup-and-fix engagement (claim, categories, photos, services, description) that delivers a fast, visible before/after.
- **A local SEO retainer** — ongoing posts, review generation, review responses, and citation upkeep, billed monthly. This is your bread-and-butter recurring income.
- **Reputation management** — building a steady stream of genuine reviews and handling the occasional angry one-star with a cool head.
- **Citation building & cleanup** — getting the client's Name, Address, Phone identical across JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing and the rest.
- **Local reporting** — turning "calls and direction requests from your profile went up" into a number the owner actually feels.
