This is the module's real deliverable — the one that produces your fastest, most screenshot-able proof of value. You're going to take your capstone client's local presence from wherever it is now to **client-ready**, and document the before/after so the win is undeniable.

## The brief

Produce three things for your capstone client, live and working:

1. A **fully optimised, compliant Google Business Profile** — verified, correctly categorised, complete.
2. A **consistent citation set** — NAP identical across the Google profile and the key India directories.
3. A **running review system** — a request script, a one-tap link/QR, and responses to existing reviews.

Plus a short **before/after document** that turns your work into evidence, and the **review-request assets** the owner can keep using.

## Steps / checklist

Work in this order — each step builds on the last.

1. **Capture the "before."** Screenshot the current profile, current review count/rating, current insights (calls, direction requests), and run the three location searches from the ranking-factors lesson. This is your baseline — you can't show progress without it.
2. **Claim / verify (or take control of) the GBP.** Get through verification. If it already exists and you have access, confirm you can edit.
3. **Optimise the core profile:**
   - Business name = real signage name, **zero keyword stuffing**.
   - Most specific accurate **primary category** + relevant secondary categories.
   - All **services/products** listed with short descriptions.
   - **Attributes** ticked truthfully (payments, accessibility, women-led, etc.).
   - **Hours** set, plus **holiday/special hours** planned.
   - **Service areas** (only if the business serves at the customer's location).
   - Human, **keyword-natural description**.
   - **Photos:** exterior, interior, team, work — aim for 10+.
4. **Audit & fix NAP + build citations.** Run the name/phone/address searches, log every listing in a sheet with a Status column, standardise the canonical NAP from the profile, fix inconsistencies, and build/claim the key India citations (Google, Bing Places, Apple, JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, socials, one industry directory, the website footer).
5. **Install the review system.** Grab the profile's short review link, shorten it, generate a QR code, write the WhatsApp/SMS request script in the client's voice, and **respond to every existing review** (good and bad).
6. **Start a Posts cadence.** Publish the first post and lay out a month's calendar (offer → value → news → proof). Seed 2–3 Q&A entries.
7. **Capture the "after"** (or the projected/early after) and write it up.

## Deliverable format

- **The live optimised profile** — the real artefact, working in Search/Maps.
- **A short before/after doc** (1–2 pages or a slide or two): before screenshots + baseline numbers, what you changed, after screenshots, and the metrics to watch (calls, direction requests, reviews, rating). Honest framing — early wins plus what will keep improving over the next weeks.
- **The review-request assets** — the script, the short link, and the printable QR — handed to the owner as a reusable kit.
- **The NAP/citation audit sheet** — Site / URL / Name / Address / Phone / Status.

## Self-assessment rubric

Grade yourself honestly — this is what a paying client would judge.

| Criterion | Missing | Getting there | Client-ready |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Profile complete & compliant** | Half-filled; or name is keyword-stuffed / against guidelines | Most fields done, a few gaps; compliant | Every field complete, verified, fully guideline-compliant, 10+ photos |
| **Categories correct** | Wrong or too-broad primary (e.g. "Doctor") | Reasonable primary, secondaries thin | Most specific accurate primary + relevant, non-spammy secondaries |
| **NAP consistent + citations built** | Inconsistencies unaddressed; few/no citations | Google + a couple of directories consistent | NAP identical across Google + key India citations; audit sheet done |
| **Review system live** | No system; or reviews ignored | Script + link exist; some reviews answered | One-tap link/QR + script deployed; **every** review responded to; ethical (no fakes/gating) |
| **Posts started** | None | One post, no plan | First post live + a month's calendar + seeded Q&A |

Aim for **Client-ready** across the board before you call it done. Anything at **Missing** is a gap a client would feel.
