Here's the module nobody puts on their Instagram. No before-and-after ranking screenshots, no "we 10x'd their traffic" flex. Just you, a fresh cup of chai, and the quietly professional act of installing a scoreboard *before* the match starts.

This is the stage of the gig where amateurs and pros split. The amateur signs the client, gets excited, and immediately "starts doing SEO." Three months later the client asks "so, is it working?" and the amateur has… vibes. A feeling. Maybe a rank-tracker screenshot from a tool the client can't see. The pro? The pro spent week one wiring up measurement, took a baseline snapshot, and can now point at a chart and say "here's where you were, here's where you are, here's the money that came from it." One of these freelancers gets referred. Guess which.

You are here in the arc because you've landed the client (Module 1), audited the site (Module 2), and built a strategy (Module 3). Now — before a single title tag gets rewritten or a rupee gets spent on Ads — you instrument. If you can't measure it, you can't bill for it.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **"Analytics setup"** as a standalone paid service — GA4 + Search Console + conversion tracking, done properly, for clients who currently have nothing or have a broken half-install.
- **A monthly reporting retainer** that's actually defensible, because the numbers trace back to a real baseline you captured.
- **"Conversion tracking"** specifically — most local businesses have never once measured a phone-click or a WhatsApp tap. You'll wire up the actions that equal money.
- **Diagnosing** why a client's existing analytics "shows nothing useful" (spoiler: it's usually counting pageviews and calling them wins).

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Explain *why* baselining and attribution come before optimisation — and defend it to a sceptical client.
- Stand up **Google Analytics 4** from scratch, correctly (INR, IST, internal traffic excluded, data flowing).
- Decide when to use **Google Tag Manager** and when it's overkill for a five-page shop site.
- Set up the **key events / conversions** an SMB actually cares about — calls, WhatsApp taps, form fills, direction requests.
- Verify and read **Google Search Console** — the free tool that shows real Google search performance.
- Tag your campaigns with **UTMs** so you get credit for the traffic you drive — without breaking your own data.

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is a **live measurement setup + a baseline snapshot** for your capstone client: GA4 firing, Search Console verified with a sitemap submitted, the money-conversions defined and tested, and a one-page "here's where we started" document. That baseline is the reference line every later module measures against. Lose it and every future win becomes an argument. Capture it and every future win becomes a receipt.

## Tools & level

**Tools:** Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Search Console, the Campaign URL Builder, and your client's Google Business Profile. All free.

**Level:** Practitioner, with a few 🎓 Foundations explainers for anyone who's never opened GA4. This isn't hard. It's just fiddly, and doing it *correctly* is the entire value — a sloppy install is worse than none, because it lies to you with confidence.
