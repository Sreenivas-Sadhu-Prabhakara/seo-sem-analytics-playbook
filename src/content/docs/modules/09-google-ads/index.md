Everything you've done so far in this gig has been the long game. On-page fixes, local SEO, backlinks — you're planting a mango tree that fruits in six months. Wonderful. Except your client runs a two-chair dental clinic in Jayanagar and rent is due on the fifth.

This module is the switch on the wall. Flip it, and your client is on top of Google's results for "root canal near me" by lunchtime. That's the magic of paid search — instant, controllable, measurable traffic. It's also the danger, because every click costs real rupees, and a badly built campaign can vaporise a month's ad budget in a weekend while you're at a wedding. SEO wastes your time when it goes wrong. Ads waste your client's money — and there's no faster way to lose a client than to lose their money.

Done right, Google Ads management is one of the best-paid services a freelancer can offer, precisely *because* it's high-stakes and most people are scared of it. You're not just a technician. You're the person the owner trusts with the debit card. That trust has a price tag.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **Building and launching Search campaigns** for local businesses — the bread-and-butter paid service.
- **Ongoing ads management** — a monthly retainer or a percentage of ad spend, because campaigns need weekly tending, not one-time setup.
- **Conversion tracking setup** — wiring Google Ads to GA4 so the client can see leads, not just clicks. Most cheap "ad guys" skip this. You won't.
- **Rescue jobs** — auditing and fixing a campaign someone else built badly. There are a *lot* of these.
- **Paid + organic strategy** — advising when to rent traffic and when to build it, so the client stops overspending.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Decide whether a business should advertise at all, and on which keywords.
- Structure a Google Ads account cleanly — account, campaigns, ad groups, keywords.
- Choose match types and build a negative-keyword list that stops wasted spend.
- Write Responsive Search Ads and add the assets (extensions) that lift click-through rate.
- Set a sensible daily budget and a starting bid strategy for a thin-data account.
- Wire conversion tracking and point clicks at a landing page that actually converts.
- Spend responsibly — cap budgets, pause losers, and report what the money returned.

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is a **fully-built Search campaign for your capstone client, with conversion tracking wired in** — launched if the budget allows, or staged ready-to-go if not — plus a one-page campaign plan. This is the fastest, hardest proof in the whole course: leads at a known cost. When you hand the capstone report over in Module 11, "we generated 14 enquiries at ₹190 each" hits differently than "impressions are up."

## Tools and level

**Tools:** Google Ads, GA4 (for conversion import), Google Keyword Planner, and whatever landing page the client already has. **Dominant level: Practitioner.** You need the Foundations from earlier modules — especially [measurement](/seo-sem-analytics-playbook/modules/04-measurement/) — because ads without conversion tracking is just donating to Google.
