Time to stop reading about it and build it. Your assignment is the real deliverable of this module — a **complete Google Ads Search campaign for your capstone client, with conversion tracking wired in.** If the client has a budget, you launch it. If not, you build it fully and stage it, ready to go live the moment they say yes. Either way, you finish this module with a professional campaign you can screenshot into your portfolio and point at in the capstone report.

This is the paid-results proof that makes the whole capstone land. "We generated enquiries at a known cost" is the most persuasive line in any freelancer's report.

## The brief

Produce a fully-built Search campaign a paying client would accept, plus a one-page plan explaining it — structured, targeted, tracked, and pointed at a landing page that can actually convert. No junk-drawer ad groups, no broad-match-with-no-negatives, no untracked spend.

## Steps / checklist

Work through these in order — each builds on the last:

1. **Pick the target.** Choose the single service most worth advertising (lesson 1) and confirm the maths works — high enough intent and margin to justify paid clicks.
2. **Choose high-intent keywords.** 5–8 tight, ready-to-buy keywords **per ad group**, in **phrase and exact** match to start (lesson 3). Group them into 2–4 single-theme ad groups.
3. **Set the geography.** A small radius or specific localities — only where the client actually serves (lesson 5). Add neighbouring areas they *don't* serve as negatives.
4. **Structure the campaign.** One campaign, 2–4 tightly-themed ad groups, named in the `Search — Service — City` pattern (lesson 2).
5. **Write the ads.** One RSA per ad group — at least 8 varied headlines (benefit / intent / local / CTA) and 3 descriptions (lesson 4). Check message match to the keyword and the landing page.
6. **Add assets.** Sitelinks, callouts, and — for any local business — a **call asset** at minimum; add location if there's a storefront (lesson 4).
7. **Set negatives.** Apply your negative-keyword list (start from the swipe list in lesson 3, add client-specific junk).
8. **Set budget and bidding.** Daily budget = `monthly ÷ 30.4`, a **max-CPC cap**, and **Maximize Clicks** to start (no conversion history yet) — lesson 5.
9. **Wire conversion tracking.** Import your GA4 **key events** (form, call, WhatsApp) into Google Ads as conversions, and confirm a test action registers (lesson 6). *This step is non-negotiable.*
10. **Sort the landing page.** Point each ad group at a fast, mobile, message-matched page with one clear CTA — build a dedicated page if only a generic homepage exists (lesson 6).
11. **Run the pre-launch checklist** (the swipe in lesson 6). Every box ticked before go-live.
12. **Launch or stage.** If budget allows, launch and set a reminder to review in 3–7 days. If not, save it ready and note exactly what "go live" requires.

## Deliverable format

Two things:

1. **The built campaign** — screenshots or exports showing the structure (campaign → ad groups → keywords), the RSA(s), assets, negatives, budget/bidding settings, and conversion actions. If launched, include an early performance screenshot.
2. **A one-page campaign plan** covering the target service and why, the keywords and match types, the geo and budget, the RSA headlines, the negatives, the conversion actions tracked, the landing page, and **expected CPC/CPA as a caveated range** — clearly labelled illustrative, noting that the live auction sets the real price and two weeks of data will replace the estimate. Promise no lead counts.

## Simulated Client fallback

*(Hypothetical brief — clearly fictional, for readers without a real client.)*

**Build the full campaign in a draft or practice account without spending a rupee.** Google Ads lets you build campaigns and leave them un-launched, so you can complete every step above except the live spend.

> **Simulated client:** *Sunrise Physiotherapy, a composite two-therapist clinic near a metro station in a tier-2 city. Main service: back and knee pain physiotherapy. Budget: ~₹9,000/month. Wants phone enquiries and clinic visits. Has a basic homepage and a Google Business Profile.*

Build Sunrise's campaign end-to-end — keywords like `physiotherapy for back pain [area]`, tight geo, an RSA, a call asset, negatives (`jobs`, `course`, `free`, other areas), a ₹300/day Maximize-Clicks setup, conversion actions defined, and a proposed dedicated landing page. Leave it staged, not launched. You'll have a complete, screenshot-able campaign with zero risk.

## Self-assessment rubric

Grade yourself honestly against each criterion:

| Criterion | Missing | Getting there | Client-ready |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tight structure** | One ad group holding everything | Themed ad groups, loose naming | Single-theme ad groups, consistent `Search — Service — City` naming |
| **High-intent keywords + negatives** | Broad, vague keywords; no negatives | Decent keywords, thin negative list | 5–8 high-intent phrase/exact keywords per ad group + a real negative list (junk, jobs, wrong areas) |
| **Strong RSAs + assets** | 1–2 repetitive headlines, no assets | Several headlines, some assets | 8+ varied headlines across angles, 3+ descriptions, message-matched, key assets incl. call |
| **Conversion tracking wired** | None — spending blind | Tracking set up but untested | GA4 key events imported as conversions, test action confirmed registering |
| **Sensible budget & landing page** | Whole-city geo, homepage, random bid strategy | Reasonable budget, generic page | Tight geo, `monthly÷30.4` daily budget + max-CPC cap, Maximize Clicks, fast message-matched landing page |

Anything not yet "Client-ready" is your next hour's work. Don't hand a client — or your portfolio — a "Getting there" campaign.
