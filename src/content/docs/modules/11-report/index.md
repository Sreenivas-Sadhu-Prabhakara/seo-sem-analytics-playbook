Here's a hard truth nobody tells the new freelancer: your client cannot see your work. They can't see the title tags you rewrote at 11pm, the schema you wrestled into place, the twelve reviews you nudged out of shy customers. All of that is invisible to the person signing your invoice. What they *can* see is the report you send. As far as the client is concerned, **the report is the product.**

This is the stage of the gig where good freelancers get fired and mediocre ones get renewed — and it's almost never about the actual results. It's about whether the owner *understood and felt* the value. A brilliant campaign that the client doesn't grasp gets cancelled at the next cash crunch. A modest campaign, reported like a story with a clear hero and a happy owner, gets a raise. Reporting isn't the boring bit at the end. Reporting is how you keep getting paid.

You've landed the client, audited, strategised, wired up measurement (Module 4's baseline is about to earn its keep), executed local, on-page, technical and paid work. Now you close the loop. You take a pile of numbers only you understand and turn it into one sentence Dr. Meena repeats to her husband over dinner: *"That marketing girl got me eleven new patient calls last month."* That sentence is your renewal.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **Reporting as a line item in every retainer** — and, more importantly, as the thing that *justifies* the retainer month after month. Clients don't renew for rankings; they renew because the report made the value obvious.
- **A live, branded client dashboard** built for free — the kind agencies charge a setup fee for.
- **Monthly reporting** as a defensible recurring deliverable, not an afterthought you dread.
- **The upsell** — because a well-run results conversation is where the next project quietly sells itself.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Report the metrics that mean **money** — calls, WhatsApp enquiries, walk-ins, bookings, cost per lead — and translate marketing jargon into business language.
- Build a free, live **Looker Studio dashboard** wired to GA4, Search Console and Google Ads.
- Write a short **monthly report** that leads with the win, owns the misses honestly, and reads in two minutes.
- Run a confident **results call** — including the "why isn't it working yet?" conversation — and land the renewal.
- Turn a happy client into a **testimonial and case study** that wins your next client, without ever inflating a number.

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is a **client-ready Looker Studio dashboard plus a one-to-two-page monthly report** for your capstone client, built on the baseline you captured in Module 4. This pair — the living dashboard and the story wrapped around it — becomes the centrepiece of your portfolio case study.

## Tools & level

**Tools:** Looker Studio (free), GA4, Google Search Console, Google Ads, Google Business Profile insights, and a plain document. All free.

**Level:** Freelancer. This is less about new software and more about *communication* — the skill that separates a technician who gets replaced from a consultant who gets referred.
