Back in Module 4 you installed the scoreboard. GA4 firing, conversions defined, Search Console verified, a baseline dated and saved. Then for six modules you played the match — local SEO, on-page, technical, backlinks, Ads. The whole time, quietly, the scoreboard has been keeping count.

Now you read it.

This is the module that separates two kinds of freelancer, and they charge very different rates. The first kind "does SEO," feels busy, and when the client asks "so, is it working?" reaches for vibes and a rank-tracker screenshot. The second kind opens GA4 on a Monday morning, sees that three pages get impressions but nobody clicks, rewrites their titles by lunch, and watches the line move. Same tools. Wildly different income. The difference is that the second freelancer treats data as an instruction, not a trophy.

Because here's the thing nobody tells you when you're learning analytics: **a number that doesn't change what you do next is entertainment, not insight.** You can stare at a beautiful dashboard for an hour and be no richer for it. The reader who finishes this module doesn't admire the data — they interrogate it, act on it, and write down what happened. That loop, run every week for a year, is what quietly compounds into a client who never leaves.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **Ongoing optimisation** — the fat, recurring middle of a monthly retainer. Setup is a one-time fee; *this* is the reason the retainer renews.
- **"Analytics reviews"** — a monthly read-out of what the numbers mean and what you changed because of them. Owners will pay just to finally understand their own data.
- **Rescue diagnosis** — walking into an account someone else built, reading it, and finding the leak in twenty minutes.
- **Cross-tool clarity** — being the one person who can explain why Ads, GA4 and Search Console all show different "lead" numbers, and which one to trust.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Open GA4 and go straight to the four reports that matter for an SMB — without drowning in the other forty.
- Explain attribution honestly: what "last click" hides, why a channel can be assisting invisibly, and why no single number deserves blind trust.
- Join Google Ads, GA4 and Search Console into one picture without double-counting your leads.
- Run a repeatable **weekly optimisation loop** — review, hypothesise, change *one* thing, measure — for SEO, Ads and local all at once.
- Test and learn on tiny budgets and thin traffic without lying to yourself about "winners."

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is an **optimisation log**: a simple table documenting three or more data-driven changes you made for the capstone client — the observation, the change, the hypothesis, and what you'll check next. It's small and it's mighty. It's the artefact that proves, to the client and to your next client, that you don't guess. You read, you change one thing, you measure, you repeat. That log is the receipt for the word "improve."

## Tools and level

**Tools:** Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Ads, and the account links between them. All free. The only paid input is your attention.

**Level:** Practitioner. You'll lean on the [measurement](/seo-sem-analytics-playbook/modules/04-measurement/) Foundations — this module assumes the scoreboard is already wired and reading it is the job now.
