You've signed the client. The contract is in your inbox, the advance has hit your UPI, and there's a small, dangerous voice in your head whispering *"just start doing stuff."* Post on their Google Business Profile. Rewrite a title tag. Launch an ad. Look busy.

Resist it. A doctor who writes a prescription before checking your pulse isn't confident — they're negligent. And in freelancing, prescribing before diagnosing doesn't just produce bad work; it produces a client who, three months in, asks *"so what exactly have you been doing?"* — and you have no answer, because you never had a baseline.

This module turns you into a diagnostician. You'll walk into every new engagement with a system: interview the owner, health-check the website, grade the SEO and local presence, audit whether anything is even being measured, and size up the paid and competitive landscape. Then you package it all into one document — the **Audit Report** — that does something magical: it makes the client *see* their own problems, agree they're real, and ask you to fix them.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **Paid audits as a standalone product.** Many owners aren't ready for a retainer, but they'll happily pay for a clear diagnosis. The audit becomes a low-risk first sale that de-risks the bigger one.
- **Discovery calls that don't feel like interrogations** — structured conversations that surface the real business, the real goals, and the one metric that actually pays their bills.
- **A repeatable diagnostic system** you run in a day or two, not a week, using free tools.
- **Opportunity-framed reporting** that turns "your site is a mess" into "here are five things we fix, in priority order, starting Monday."

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Run a discovery interview that uncovers goals, margins, seasonality and past marketing burns.
- Do a fast technical health-check of any small-business website with free tools.
- Grade a business's organic and local search presence against real competitors.
- Tell whether GA4, Search Console and conversion tracking exist — and document exactly what's missing.
- Spot wasted ad spend and read competitor strategy straight off the search results page.
- Assemble a prioritised, client-ready Audit Report that upsells the next step.

## The deliverable

The module Assignment is a complete **Audit Report** for your capstone client — executive summary, findings by area, prioritised opportunities, and a recommended plan with illustrative pricing. It's the document that defines everything you'll fix in Modules 4 through 9, and it's the first real artefact in your freelance portfolio.

## Tools used

Google Business Profile, plain Google Search, PageSpeed Insights, Google Search Console, GA4 (checking for its presence), the Ads Transparency Center, and a blank document. Almost all free. The dominant level here is **Practitioner** — you're doing real fieldwork on a real business.
