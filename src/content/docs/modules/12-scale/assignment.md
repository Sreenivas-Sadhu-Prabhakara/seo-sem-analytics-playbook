This is it — the last assignment of the whole course, and the one that turns everything you've learned into an actual income. It has three parts, and together they *are* the capstone: the **proof** of what you can do, and the **plan** to go do it again for money. Finish this, and you don't have a certificate of completion — you have a portfolio, a certification schedule, and a client-acquisition machine.

## The brief

Produce three artefacts for your real capstone client (Meena's Dental Clinic in our running example; *your* actual client in reality):

1. A **portfolio case study** — a one-page story of the results you delivered.
2. A **certification plan** — 1–2 free Google certs, picked and scheduled.
3. A **90-day client-acquisition plan** — an income goal and the weekly actions to hit it.

## The steps

### Part 1 — Assemble the case study

Pull together the work you did across Modules 2–11 into a single one-pager, using the outline from the credibility-stack lesson:

- **Situation** — where the client started (invisible on Maps, no tracking, thin site — whatever was true).
- **What you did** — 4–6 bullets spanning the audit, local SEO/Profile, on-page, technical, GA4 setup, Ads and reporting.
- **Results** — the real numbers from your Module 11 dashboard: Maps calls, ranking movement, tracked bookings, cost-per-lead. **Real or client-approved figures only** — no invented stats.
- **Client quote** — the testimonial you gathered in Module 11, in their words, with permission.

### Part 2 — Pick and schedule your certifications

On **Google Skillshop** (free), choose **1–2** certifications that match your work — the **Google Ads Search** certification and/or the **Google Analytics (GA4)** certification are the natural picks. Put real exam dates on your calendar within the next 90 days. A plan with a date is a commitment; a plan without one is a wish.

### Part 3 — Write your 90-day acquisition plan

Set a specific **monthly income goal.** Work backwards: divide by your average retainer to get the client count you need, then multiply up through your conversion rates to size the conversations and leads (the method from the acquisition-engine lesson). Then write your **weekly rhythm** — your realistic dose of referrals, outreach, content and marketplace activity — and set up your one-tab pipeline tracker.

## Deliverable format

Three tidy pieces you can actually use:

- **A portfolio one-pager / case study** (a clean PDF — situation, work, results, quote).
- **A cert schedule** (which certs, which dates, on your calendar).
- **A 90-day acquisition plan** (income goal, backward math, weekly actions, plus your live pipeline tracker).

## Simulated Client fallback

No real capstone client? Build all three from your training work — **clearly labelled a training project, not a paid client engagement.** Write the case study from the fictional-but-realistic business you practised on throughout the course, using derived or clearly-illustrative results (and say so on the page — honesty is the whole point). The certs and the 90-day plan are entirely real regardless; do those exactly as written. A labelled training case study still demonstrates your skills honestly, and it's replaced by a real one the moment you land client #1.

## Self-assessment rubric

Grade yourself honestly against each criterion.

| Criterion | Missing | Getting there | Client-ready |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Case study** | No case study, or vague claims with no numbers | Situation + work described, but results are thin or unquantified | Clear situation → work → **real/derived results** → client quote, on one clean page |
| **Cert plan** | No certs chosen | Certs chosen but not scheduled | 1–2 relevant free certs picked **with real exam dates** on the calendar |
| **90-day plan** | "Get more clients," no goal or actions | Income goal set but no backward math or weekly rhythm | **Income goal + backward math + concrete weekly actions** and a live pipeline tracker |

Aim for "Client-ready" in all three. Anything less is a to-do list, not a graduation.

## How this feeds your capstone

There is no bigger deliverable after this — **this *is* the capstone.** The case study is the proof that wins client #2. The cert plan is the credibility that gets you in the room. The 90-day plan is the engine that keeps the room full. Put together, they're the difference between "I once did some SEO for a dentist" and "I run a freelance search-marketing practice." This is the artefact you point strangers at for the rest of your career.
