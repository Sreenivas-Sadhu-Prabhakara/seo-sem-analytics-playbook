The map pack got them seen. Now the website has to close.

Here's the split nobody explains to the client: **local SEO gets you found; on-page SEO gets you chosen.** When someone searches "physiotherapy near me" and taps your client's listing, they land on a page. That page has about eight seconds to prove it's the right clinic before the thumb swipes back to the results and taps the competitor. On-page SEO is where you win — or lose — that eight seconds, twice over: once with Google's crawler deciding what the page is even *about*, and once with a human in pain deciding whether to call.

That's the whole game of this module. **Every page has two audiences — a robot and a buyer — and you serve both without compromising either.** Do it right and the same page that ranks also converts. Do it lazily and you get the worst outcome in SEO: a beautifully optimised page that ranks page one and generates exactly zero phone calls.

Throughout this module we'll work on a composite capstone client — **Revive Physiotherapy, a hypothetical two-branch clinic in Indiranagar and HSR Layout, Bengaluru.** (Composite means we invented it to teach with; treat it as a stand-in for your real client.) Physio is a lovely teaching case: multiple services, two locations, and it's a health business — so the trust bar is high and every lesson lands harder.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- **Page optimisation packages** — rewriting titles, meta descriptions, headings and body copy on a client's money pages so they rank and read like a human wrote them.
- **Service & location page builds** — the structured pages that actually earn SMB leads, one per service and per branch.
- **Content plans & calendars** — a 90-day, intent-matched content roadmap the client can follow (or pay you to execute).
- **Conversion-focused copy** — turning "we do physio" into "book your assessment today," with the call/WhatsApp/form in the right place.
- **Quality-controlled AI content** — using AI to draft faster without shipping the generic slop that Google and humans both punish.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this module you can:

- Audit and rewrite the on-page elements of any page — title tag, meta description, headings, URL, internal links, alt text — from a repeatable checklist.
- Build service pages and location pages that are genuinely useful and unique, not thin doorway spam that gets a site penalised.
- Match page type to search intent and structure content so a skimming, mobile, in-a-hurry buyer converts.
- Run an AI-draft-plus-human-edit workflow that produces fast, accurate, local content instead of AI slop.
- Signal E-E-A-T — real experience, expertise, authority and trust — the way a small local business credibly can.

## The deliverable

Your module Assignment: **optimise your capstone client's top 3–5 money pages and hand them a 90-day content calendar.** Rewritten titles, meta, headings and copy; a clear local CTA on every page; and a realistic content roadmap. This is the on-page half of the results you'll show in the final capstone report — the pages that turn rankings into rupees.

## Tools & level

- **Tools:** the client's website (or a page-by-page recommendation doc if you lack access), your keyword/intent map from Module 3, a spreadsheet for the content calendar, and an AI assistant used *carefully*.
- **Level:** mostly **Practitioner**. A couple of Foundations explainers for beginners, and a few Level-up plays once you're confident.
