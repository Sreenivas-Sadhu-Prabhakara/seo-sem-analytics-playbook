You've finished the audit. You know what's broken — the missing title tags, the ghost-town Google Business Profile, the website that loads slower than a Monday morning. You've got a document full of problems.

And here's the trap almost every new freelancer falls into: they hand the owner that pile of problems and say "so… shall we fix everything?" The owner's eyes glaze over, they mutter "let me think about it," and you never hear back. You didn't lose the deal on skill. You lost it because a list of problems is not a plan.

This module is where you become worth paying. An audit tells the owner what's *wrong*. A strategy tells them **what to do about it, in what order, for the money they actually have.** Strategy is the art of choosing what NOT to do — because your client can't afford to do everything, and the ones who try to do everything achieve nothing. You're going to pick the two or three moves that matter, sequence them, and put a number on the outcome you can honestly promise.

The backbone of all of it is **keyword research** — figuring out the exact words your client's customers type into Google at the moment they're ready to spend. Get that right and every later module (the on-page work, the local SEO, the ads) has a target to aim at. Get it wrong and you'll rank beautifully for words nobody searches.

## What you'll be able to charge for after this

- A **paid strategy / roadmap** as a standalone deliverable — many freelancers give this away; you'll learn to sell it as the first, trust-building engagement.
- A **keyword map** the client can literally see their business inside — themes, intent, and target pages, not a random dump of 500 phrases.
- A **90-day plan** that splits a small budget sensibly between slow-compounding SEO and fast-but-rented ads.
- **Honest forecasts** that set expectations you can actually hit — the thing that keeps clients past month one.

## By the end of this module you can

- Run keyword research using only free tools (Keyword Planner, autocomplete, Trends).
- Read a keyword's *intent* off the live search results and match it to the right page type.
- Handle the messy Indian reality: "near me," Hinglish, transliteration, landmark searches, voice.
- Prioritise every task on an effort-vs-impact matrix and defend the sequence.
- Set goals tied to leads and rupees — never to rankings — and forecast honestly.
- Write a skimmable strategy doc an owner signs off in one reading.

## The deliverable

Your Assignment is a **strategy document plus a keyword map** for your capstone client — the exact artefact you'd put in front of a paying owner to get a signature before any real work begins. This roadmap becomes the marching orders for Modules 4 through 9: everything you execute later traces back to a line in this plan.

:::note[🎓 Foundations]
New here? "Strategy" isn't a fancy word for "to-do list." A to-do list is *everything you could do*. A strategy is *the few things you will do, in order, and the many things you're deliberately skipping* — with a reason for each choice. If you can't say what you're NOT doing, you don't have a strategy yet.
:::

**Tools used:** Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, Google autocomplete, a spreadsheet, and a plain document. **Dominant level:** Practitioner — you'll do real client work here, not theory.
